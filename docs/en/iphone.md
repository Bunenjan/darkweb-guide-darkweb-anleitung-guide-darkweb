# Tor on iPhone and iPad

[← Back to overview](../../README.md)

**Important:** There is **no official Tor Browser** for iPhone and iPad. Instead, the Tor Project recommends the **Onion Browser** app. It is open source, uses the Tor network and is developed by someone who works closely with the Tor Project.

**Requirement:** iOS 15 or later.

## Limitation

Apple requires all browsers on iPhone to use a technology called WebKit. Because of this, Onion Browser **does not have the same protections** as Tor Browser on a computer or on Android. The EFF advises against using it for highly sensitive browsing. If you need more protection, use a computer.

## Installation

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **Onion Browser**.
3. Check the seller: the app is by **Mike Tigas**.
4. Tap **Get**.

> **Warning:** The App Store has many apps with "Tor" in their name, often paid or with a built-in VPN. Only **Onion Browser** is recommended by the Tor Project.

## First start

1. Open **Onion Browser**.
2. Follow the on-screen steps and connect to the Tor network.
3. Open [check.torproject.org](https://check.torproject.org). It must say **"Congratulations"**.

## Security

- Look for the security options in the app's settings and choose the strictest level with which your sites still work.
- The rules of behaviour from the [overview](../../README.md#rules-of-behaviour) apply here too.

## Optional: Orbot

On iOS, the Tor Project also recommends the **Orbot** app. It lets other apps run through Tor as well. For getting started, Onion Browser is enough.

## Uninstall

Press and hold the app icon → **Remove App** → **Delete App**.
