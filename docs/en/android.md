# Tor Browser on Android (e.g. Samsung)

[← Back to overview](../../README.md)

This guide applies to all Android phones, including Samsung Galaxy.

> **Warning:** If you search the Play Store for "Tor Browser", you will find many third-party apps, often with ads or a built-in VPN. Tor Browser for Android is officially available only on the Play Store, F-Droid and the Tor Project website. According to the Tor Project, downloading it anywhere else is very risky.

## Option A: Google Play Store (easiest)

1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Search for **Tor Browser**.
3. Check that the developer is **The Tor Project**.
4. Tap **Install**.

## Option B: F-Droid

F-Droid is an app store for free, open-source apps. Tor Browser is provided there by the Guardian Project.

1. Download the F-Droid app from [f-droid.org](https://f-droid.org) and install it.
2. Open F-Droid and enable the **Guardian Project** repository under **Repositories** in the settings.
3. Search for **Tor Browser for Android** and tap **Install**.

Detailed steps: [Tor Browser Manual: Android](https://tb-manual.torproject.org/mobile-tor)

## First start

1. Open the **Tor Browser** app.
2. Tap **Connect** and wait until the connection is established.
3. Open [check.torproject.org](https://check.torproject.org). It must say **"Congratulations"**.

## Set the security level

Android has the same three levels as the desktop version (Standard, Safer, Safest).

1. Tap the **three-dot menu**.
2. Open **Settings** and look for **Security Level**.
3. Choose **Safer** or **Safest**.

## Updates

Updates arrive automatically through the Play Store or F-Droid. Still, check now and then whether a new version is available.

## Uninstall

Settings → Apps → **Tor Browser** → Uninstall. Or directly in the Play Store or F-Droid.
