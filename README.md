# Into the Dark Web – Safely, Step by Step

Language: **English** · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Français](README.fr.md)

A simple guide to getting into the dark web safely: first install Tor Browser on your device, then visit your first .onion sites. For Windows, macOS, Linux, Android (e.g. Samsung) and iPhone.

## How to proceed

1. **Install Tor Browser:** choose your device below.
2. **Set the security level:** explained in every guide.
3. **Enter the dark web:** [First steps on the dark web](docs/en/darkweb.md)

## What is the dark web?

The dark web is the part of the internet you can only reach with special software such as Tor. Addresses there end in `.onion`. A normal browser like Chrome or Safari cannot open them.

The dark web has perfectly normal sites (newspapers, search engines, email services), but also a lot of scams and illegal content. That is why it is important to follow the rules in this guide.

## What is Tor?

Tor Browser routes your connection through several servers in the Tor network. Websites cannot see where you are coming from, and your internet provider cannot see which sites you visit.

Using Tor is legal in Switzerland and most other countries. What is illegal is what you do with it, exactly as on the normal internet.

> This is an unofficial guide. The official pages of the Tor Project always take precedence: [torproject.org](https://www.torproject.org) and the [Tor Browser Manual](https://tb-manual.torproject.org).

## The most important rule: official sources only

There are many fake "Tor" apps and downloads containing malware or ads. Only download from these places:

- **Windows, macOS, Linux:** [torproject.org/download](https://www.torproject.org/download/)
- **Android:** Google Play Store, F-Droid (Guardian Project) or the Tor Project website
- **iPhone:** "Onion Browser" in the App Store (recommended by the Tor Project)

## Step 2: Set the security level

Tor Browser has three security levels. By default it is set to **Standard**.

| Level | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | All features are enabled. |
| Safer | Dangerous features are disabled. JavaScript is disabled on non-HTTPS sites. Audio and video are click-to-play. |
| Safest | JavaScript is disabled everywhere. Some images and fonts are disabled. Only simple sites work. |

For beginners, **Safer** or **Safest** is recommended. Every guide explains how to change the level.

## Rules of behaviour

The biggest danger is not the technology but your own behaviour.

- **Do not download anything or open files.** PDFs and Office files can connect outside of Tor or contain malware.
- **Never enter real data:** no name, no email address, no passwords.
- **Do not install add-ons or extensions.**
- **Do not buy anything.** Many dark web shops are scams, and much of what is sold is illegal.
- **Do not click links from unknown lists** (TikTok, Discord, "dark web link lists").
- **Not on work or school devices** and not on a company network. This usually breaks IT policies.
- **Install updates immediately** when the browser asks.

## Test whether Tor works

Open [check.torproject.org](https://check.torproject.org) in Tor Browser. If it says **"Congratulations"**, your connection goes through Tor.
